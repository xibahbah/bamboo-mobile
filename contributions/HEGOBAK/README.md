# HEGOBAK — Bamboo contributions

[HEGOBAK](https://github.com/HEGOBAK) contributed the product concept, behavior requirements, and design direction for **page two: donation and bamboo growth**, working with the team on a minimal, calm experience.

## Contributions

- Defined the core flow: use the donation amount already selected in Settings, press one button, and watch the bamboo grow after a successful donation.
- Specified the growth cycle: a small shoot on day 1; stem growth without leaves through day 15; leaves developing on days 16–30; a new bamboo starting on day 31 while completed plants remain. Each plant takes 30 growth days and may have a different mature height.
- Proposed the accumulated bamboo forest, a normal view focused on the growing plant, and a wide view of the whole forest.
- Defined matching ground and environment themes, including desert, beach, forest, soil, Mars, and Jupiter.
- Directed and reviewed AI-assisted local prototypes, refining the experience toward a fixed viewpoint, a streak indicator, a green circular donation button without an amount label, and only three scene controls: donate, normal/wide view, and environment selection.
- Prepared and revised a teammate handoff describing the page's scope, growth rules, integration needs, and completion checks. Clarified that the visual teammate owns final artwork, style, animation, and visual implementation.

## Scope and implementation status

This contribution focuses on the donation-and-growth page. It does not claim authorship of teammates' home page, settings, backend, or final visual work.

The local prototypes explored and tested the proposed behavior; their implementation is not included in this documentation-only commit. This README records the contribution and does not imply that every described requirement is already implemented on the shared branch. Real payments and authoritative daily growth remain separate integration work.
