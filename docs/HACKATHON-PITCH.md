# Bamboo — Two-minute hackathon pitch

**Language:** English. **Length:** approximately two minutes at a conversational pace.

[Open the home feed.]

What if ten cents could help us feel more connected to the people around us?

We built Bamboo around a simple belief: you should not have to be wealthy to practice generosity.

Our inspiration is the bamboo coin bank tradition associated with Tzu Chi: small contributions, made with care, can become something meaningful together.

But this is a humanities project as much as a giving app. People are more than a description of what they need. They have family recipes, traditions, memories, and stories worth hearing.

[Open a community member’s profile and show Recipes or Culture.]

In Bamboo, organizations can share needs and updates, while individuals can share their own stories. Sharing is optional. Receiving support should never require someone to perform gratitude or reveal private experiences.

From a story, you can open a small giving action. The amount is visible, and each completed gift appears in Transaction History.

[Show the grove.]

Your bamboo reflects consistency, not wealth. The first successful gift of a day adds one segment. Giving more that same day does not make it grow faster. Fourteen giving days complete one bamboo; the grove grows to six stalks, then gains leaves.

A brief floating heart acknowledges each gift. The heart sends a little love toward your bamboo without making another donation. Growth animates on your first grove visit of the day.

[Open Community.]

The Community wall makes care visible. Every portrait is the same size. Members choose whether to appear, and the wall requires at least a fifteen-day streak. You can switch communities, but we never show a member’s precise location or rank people by how much they give.

Today, this is an interactive prototype: gifts are simulated, portraits are fictional, and messages stay in the browser. Real payments, verified organizations, and server-backed consent are the next steps.

Bamboo asks a small question: what kindness can I practice today?

Our hope is that many small answers can help a city feel more like a community.

## Demo sequence

1. Home → a personal profile → Recipes / Culture.
2. Home → Give on a story → one simulated gift.
3. You → Settings → Transaction History.
4. Grove → send a heart; explain one growth segment per giving day.
5. Community → switch location; explain equal portraits and optional visibility.

## Short closing if time runs out

“Bamboo makes small acts of care visible, without turning generosity into a competition. Any amount can participate. Every person belongs.”

The phone home-screen widget includes a preview slider (days 1–90). It changes only the sample widget, never actual donation records or streaks.
